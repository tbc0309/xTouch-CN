# 3D-printable enclosures

[简体中文](README.md) | English | [Project home](../README.en.md)

Download and extract the matching archive, then open its STL files in your slicer. Match the complete board model rather than screen size alone.

| Board | Download | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8 V2 | [S3 enclosure](case_esp32-s3-touch-lcd-2.8-v2.zip) | `1mm`, `2mm`, and `2mm+speaker`; each includes three buttons and `case_tf` / `case_tf_usb` bodies |
| Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-4.3 | [P4 enclosure](case_esp32-p4-wifi6-touch-lcd-4.3.zip) | Four combinations of 40-pin opening and speaker provision; each includes a body and three buttons |

No CYD or LCDWIKI ES3C35P enclosure is included. The S3 enclosure does not fit ES3C35P.

Check USB, TF-card, header and speaker openings before choosing a model. Select orientation, supports, layer height and material for the structure and printer; no universal slicing profile or pre-sliced job is supplied. Disconnect power before assembly, leave room for connectors, buttons and ventilation, and keep screws and printed parts clear of exposed circuitry.
